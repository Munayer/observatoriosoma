Method. Monthly median composites of Sentinel-2 L2A surface reflectance; disturbed surface delineated by a published spectral rule with a 0.5 ha minimum mapping unit; boundary attribution against the mining cadastre with a ±25 m tolerance applied in the title holder's favour; ground movement from Sentinel-1 interferometry.
Sources. Copernicus Sentinel data (free, full and open); national mining cadastre; protected-area register; royalty declarations.
Reproducibility. Every figure carries a sealed manifest listing its inputs, algorithm versions, parameters and hashes. The manifests are chained, so removal or alteration of a past finding is detectable.
