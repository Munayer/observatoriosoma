# Licensing intent

This prototype is delivered for evaluation. The intended release licence is
**Apache-2.0** (or **EUPL-1.2** where a European public body prefers a licence written
against EU law), with submission to the Digital Public Goods registry.

Third-party components: see `docs/03-catalogues.xlsx`, sheet "Open source", which records
the licence of every component together with a risk note where one applies. One component
is explicitly excluded: MinIO's community edition entered maintenance mode and was archived
over 2025-26.

Data: Copernicus Sentinel data is free, full and open. Brazilian ANM datasets are open
government data. Third-party reference datasets carry their own licences and are cited
individually.

EU emblem: the files in `assets/eu/` are the official "Co-funded by the European Union"
emblem and funding statement (EN and PT), downloaded unmodified (only resized) from the
European Commission's download centre for visual elements. They are not covered by the
project licence; their use is governed by the Commission's operational guidelines for
recipients of EU funding and the Administrative Arrangement with the Council of Europe
(OJ C 271, 8.9.2012).

Jean Monnet Centre of Excellence logo: the files in `assets/cejm/` are the colour and white
versions of the logo of UFMG's Centro de Excelência Jean Monnet, trimmed and resized from
the centre's own files. They belong to the centre and are not covered by the project
licence. Only a Portuguese version exists, so both languages show it.
